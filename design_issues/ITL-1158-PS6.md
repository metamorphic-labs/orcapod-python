## `src/orcapod/core/sources/polling_source.py`

### PS6 — Rows of different polled batches share a record id and a source-info token
**Status:** open
**Severity:** high
**Issue:** ITL-1158

`PollingSource` builds each fetched batch on its own (`SourceStreamBuilder.build(...,
source_id=...)`, no `record_id_column`), so a row's identifier is `row_<n>`, its position
**within its batch**, and the position restarts at 0 in every batch. Rows of different
batches therefore get the same record id (`uuid5` of `"<source_id>::row_<n>"`) and the same
source-info token (`<source_id>::row_<n>::<column>`). Measured on November 15, 2026 with two
one-row batches (`id=1`, then `id=2`): both rows have record id `38e65193727a…` and the token
`polling_source:semantic_v1:…::row_0::val`. Downstream, two such rows differ in their entry
ids only through their data, and a source-info token no longer names one row.

Related, recorded on the same issue: `DictSource`, `DataFrameSource` and `ListSource` do not
accept a `record_id_column`, so their rows can only be identified by position.

How a polled row is identified is part of the developer's open design decision on source ids
and record ids (ITL-1154 follow-up); not fixed here.
