## `src/orcapod/core/referential_tags.py` — referential tags workshop slice (ITL-1023)

### RT1 — Referential tags are supported by part of the library only
**Status:** open
**Severity:** medium
**Issue:** ITL-1023

The workshop slice of referential tags (specification attached to ITL-1004) covers sources, function pods, `Join`, `GroupBy`, select and drop, renames, filters, `DropNulls`, `SemiJoin`, `Pick` and `Index`. Everything else raises "not supported with referential tags yet" when a referential tag reaches it, instead of giving a wrong answer: `Batch` (ITL-1017), `MergeJoin` (ITL-1018), storing an operator node's results in `LOG` or `REPLAY` mode, `DerivedSource` over a referential stream and saving a pipeline or job (ITL-1019), and any operator that does not declare `REFERENTIAL_TAGS_ISSUE = None` (ITL-1022). `PollingSource` keeps its tags plain. The identities of referential streams are not frozen yet. The project's issues (ITL-1009 onward) land the full design and close this entry.
