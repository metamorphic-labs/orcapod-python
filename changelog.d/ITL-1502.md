### Breaking changes and migration

- `ArrowDatabaseProtocol` and `DBConnectorProtocol` each have a required `run_scope()`: the scope of one run, which `job.run()`, `run_async()` and a job node's `run()` enter once per database. A database or connector of your own returns an empty scope (`contextlib.nullcontext()`) unless it holds something for a run.
- `DBConnectorProtocol` has a required `read_candidates(table_name, *, keys=None, equal=None)`: the rows a lookup may match, which `ConnectorArrowDatabase` then filters exactly. A connector of your own returns the whole table, as the built-in relational connectors do (`orcapod.databases.table_names.whole_table`).
