### Breaking changes and migration

- SQLite and PostgreSQL stores now name record tables using a readable path hint
  and a digest of the full path. Existing tables under the old names stay in the
  database, but Orcapod omits them from listings and reads their paths as missing.
  Rebuild an existing store from its inputs in a fresh SQLite database or
  PostgreSQL schema. Orcapod leaves the old tables untouched and does not migrate them.
