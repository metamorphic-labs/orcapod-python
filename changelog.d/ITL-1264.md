### Changed

- A dictionary-encoded tag column is refused (ITL-1264): dictionary-encoded tags are not
  currently supported in Orcapod. A tag column, a data-tag included, whose Arrow type is a
  `dictionary` or holds one inside it (a list of dictionary values, a struct with a dictionary
  field) raises `InputValidationError` when a source or a stream is built. The message names
  each such column, its type and the type it decodes to. Before, such a tag was accepted and
  failed later and far from the cause (in `as_table(all_info=True)`, a filter, `Batch`,
  `GroupBy` or a join), or ran through a job. Such columns come from an Arrow table of your
  own, a pandas `Categorical` of strings and a polars `Categorical` or `Enum`; decode the
  column first (pyarrow `column.cast(column.type.value_type)`, polars
  `pl.col(name).cast(pl.String)`). Dictionary-encoded data columns are accepted as before.
