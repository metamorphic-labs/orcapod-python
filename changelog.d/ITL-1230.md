### Added

- A documentation page, *Working in notebooks* (`docs/concepts/notebooks.md`), for the
  notebook user: what a stream, source or job node shows at the end of a cell (the preview
  table, with a picture), how to see more rows, the run summary of `job.run()`, the pipeline
  and job diagram (with a picture: statuses, a node's details, the `collapse` checkbox,
  pan, zoom and keyboard use, untrusted notebooks, `InDevelopmentWarning`), and pointers to
  running asynchronously and to classes defined in a notebook cell. The pictures are taken
  in JupyterLab, in its light and dark themes, and the site shows the one that matches its
  own theme. (ITL-1230)
