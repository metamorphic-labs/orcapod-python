### Changed

- The select and drop operators act on a referential tag by role (developer decision,
  November 16, 2026, ITL-1195), as documented in "Moving a column between roles": for a
  referential tag `x`, dropping the tag (`drop_tag_columns`) removes the tag role only
  and leaves `x` plain data with its source info; dropping the data (`drop_data_columns`)
  removes the data role only and leaves `x` a plain tag with the same values; dropping
  the column (`drop_columns`) removes it from both roles. `select_tag_columns`,
  `select_data_columns` and `select_columns` follow the same rule for the names they do
  not select. This was already the slice's behaviour; it is now the stated rule, with
  tests by role. No second name (such as `untag`) is added.
- Removing the data role of a referential tag emits no warning: it is the defined meaning
  of the call. `ReferenceMaterializationWarning`, the
  `referential_tags.reference_materialization` setting and their helpers are removed.
