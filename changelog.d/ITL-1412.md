### Fixed

- Function pipeline records are published only after their referenced results commit.
  A failed or discarded result batch withdraws its pending pipeline records, so
  later flushes cannot publish dangling references. Failed table commits identify
  the store and table. A configured no-op result database still records the processed
  inputs in the pipeline store and explicitly discards its outputs.

### Changed

- The internal `ArrowDatabaseProtocol` now requires `pending_result`,
  `guard_record`, `fail_pending_result`, `flush_result_batch`, `flush_batch`,
  and `stores_records`.
