### Added

- Per-row provenance of a job (ITL-1432). `trace` and `why_missing` are provisional in
  0.4.0: their answers may be refined; a case they cannot prove is reported unavailable or
  refused (`ProvenanceInconsistencyError`), never guessed. `diagram(highlight=...)` is in
  development.
  - `node.trace(row, column)` on a job node traces one cell back to the source cells it
    was made from. It returns a `Trace` of `TraceStep`s, with `SourceCell` leaves.
    - Each function step has a `FunctionRun`: the function's name, version and body
      hash, the executor and the time it computed, and whether this row's entry computed
      the result or reused one stored for equal inputs.
    - A `GroupBy` or `Batch` step lists its members, found by the operator's own rule and
      checked by folding their system tags (`ProvenanceInconsistencyError` if they
      disagree).
    - The trace is exact on a result shared by two input rows, and works the same on a
      loaded job after `run(load_only=True)`.
    - Where a node holds no run, the trace says `not_computed`.
    - A source is never read (a cached, polling, derived or user-defined source is
      reported `unavailable`).
    - Every value is checked against the row it came from, and an ambiguous link is
      refused.
  - `node.why_missing(source, record_id)` returns an `Absence`:
    - whether the record ever reached the first function node, from its stored pipeline
      entries;
    - the nodes holding a row from it;
    - the step that removed it, as a `Step` in `explain()`'s words;
    - for a join, the key missing from the other input.
  - `job.diagram(highlight=...)` emphasizes a `Highlight`'s nodes and edges and shows
    its note. `Trace.highlight()` and `Absence.highlight()` give the trace's or the
    missing row's path.
  - The new types are in `orcapod.types`. None of these reads computes, calls user code,
    polls or writes, and no hash, stored path or stored column changes.

### Known limitations

- Provenance (`trace`, `why_missing`) is provisional in 0.4.0 (ITL-1432). Known limits:
  - A source is never read. A cached, polling, derived or user-defined source gives
    no values for a step that needs its rows: such a step is `unavailable`. Also, the
    first step after such a source is never blamed for a missing record.
  - Only Orcapod's own operators are followed. A subclass of one, or an operator of
    your own, is `unavailable`.
  - Two joins whose inputs share one pipeline hash, joined again with their sources in
    opposite sort orders, are refused rather than guessed.
  - A stored entry that cannot tell two such tied inputs apart leaves the stored
    answer (`reached_function`) unknown.
  - After a fold, the stored answer is known only for the groups the run holds.
  - Entries of a recomputed result are told apart only once pipeline entries record
    their run id (ITL-1433).
  - Job nodes only.
