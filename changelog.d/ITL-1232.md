### Added

- **A `vertical` checkbox in the notebook diagram (in development).** Next to the `collapse` checkbox, it switches the diagram between the left-to-right and top-to-bottom layouts in place; together the two checkboxes give all four layouts. `direction=` and `collapse=` set the layout shown first and the one shown where scripts do not run. A selected node stays selected when it is in the new layout, and the new picture is fitted to the cell. The checkboxes appear for graphs of up to 100 nodes; a larger diagram carries only the layout its arguments ask for, which keeps the notebook small. (ITL-1232)

### Changed

- **Narrower nodes in the notebook diagram.** The line under a node's name (kind, what it is, status) is wrapped onto short lines instead of making the node as wide as the whole line, so the tutorial job is 1,213 pixels wide instead of 1,664 and fits a notebook cell of a laptop-sized window at a readable size. Nothing is left out of the node. (ITL-1232)
