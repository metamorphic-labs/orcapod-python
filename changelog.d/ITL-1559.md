### Added

- Streams, recorded invocations, and job nodes expose `source_chain()`, `name_chain()`, and `node_hash()`. The node hash identifies a computation by its structure and input slot names, so it is available on an unbound pipeline. Existing pipeline and content hashes keep their values.

- Saved operator descriptors can retain input symmetry so a job loaded without its operator implementation reports the same chain-based hashes as the live job.
