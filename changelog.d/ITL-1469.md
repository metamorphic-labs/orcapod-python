### Fixed

- Pipeline capture and job binding reject two in-memory sources that share a
  source ID and schema but contain different rows. With different key columns
  the rows are compared without their record IDs, as a multiset of tags and
  data. The error names the conflicting steps or slots. Job binding and loading
  also reject different schemas at a shared source ID.

### Known limitations

- Sources that share a source ID and schema but have different key columns are
  refused at capture, binding (into any slots) and loading. In-memory sources
  with the same rows are reported as differing only in key columns; the rows of
  sources read later are not compared. ITL-1506 will allow distinct readings of
  one dataset in distinct named slots.
