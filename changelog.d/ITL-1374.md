### Added

- `stream.filter(...)` and the `Filter` operator keep the rows that satisfy a condition, as a
  step of the pipeline (ITL-1374). Equalities are written as `where()` takes them
  (`units.filter(session="s1")` or a mapping); everything else with `orcapod.col`:
  `col("unit").is_in([3, 7])`, `col("t") >= 1.0`, `col("t").between(0.5, 2.0)`,
  `col("rate").is_null()`, `col("info").field("kind") != "noise"` (a field of a struct
  column), `col("a") < col("b")`, combined with `&`, `|` and `~`. Values are checked against
  their column's type and never converted; a null makes a comparison unknown and drops the
  row (SQL's rule); floats match as Orcapod matches them everywhere (`0.0` equals `-0.0`,
  NaN equals NaN and sorts above every number). The operator's identity is an order-free
  canonical form of its condition. `Filter` is provisional: its identity is not frozen yet.
- `orcapod.types.Condition` holds these conditions as a tree (`Condition.root`), with
  `canonical_form()`, and `to_config()` / `from_config()` for every kind; a condition of
  equalities only is saved as before.

### Changed

- `SpiralDBTableSource.where` refuses a condition other than equalities (a comparison,
  `is_in`, a null test, `|` or `~`) with a `TypeError` that points to `filter`; its
  equalities behave as before.
