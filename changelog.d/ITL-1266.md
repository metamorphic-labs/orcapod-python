### Added

- Provisional operators: `orcapod.operators.PROVISIONAL_OPERATORS` lists the operators whose
  identity is not frozen with data context v1 (ITL-1266). Today it holds only `PolarsFilter`
  (its identity is decided under ITL-1247). Building a provisional operator, directly or
  through a stream method such as `polars_filter`, issues `ProvisionalHashWarning` once per
  operator per process. A later release may change its identity, and with it the stored
  paths and record ids of its results and of every step downstream of it; those results are
  then computed again (nothing stored is lost or wrong). The hash stability that data context
  v1 promises from 0.4.0 holds for a pipeline only when it contains no provisional operator.
  See "Provisional operators" in `docs/concepts/operators.md`.
