### Added

- `Rejoin` (stream method `rejoin`) puts the branches of one stream back together, row by
  origin row (ITL-1336). A diamond (one stream split into branches, then put back
  together) is expected to use it: it matches every system-tag column and keeps those
  columns unchanged, so a result row has its origin row's provenance and stacked diamonds
  keep a constant number of system-tag columns, where `Join` doubles them at every diamond.
  The inputs must share their system-tag columns: a branch through `Join`, `GroupBy` or
  `Batch` is refused, pointing to `Join`. Each input holds at most one row per origin row (a
  first rule, which may be relaxed later); a user tag in several inputs must agree on
  matched rows; data columns are disjoint, as in `Join`. Two sources with the same schema
  rejoin to nothing (no shared origin row), not an error. Rejoin is commutative, takes two
  or more inputs, and is provisional (`PROVISIONAL_OPERATORS`, a `ProvisionalHashWarning`):
  its identity may still change. `Join` is unchanged.
