### Breaking changes and migration

- `DBConnectorProtocol` has three more required methods: `table_name(path)`, `has_table(table_name)` and `table_paths(prefix, pending)`. `ConnectorArrowDatabase` now asks the connector to name its tables, test their existence and list their paths. SQLite, PostgreSQL and SpiralDB keep the names and listings they had. A connector of your own implements the three methods; `orcapod.databases.table_names` has the rule the built-in connectors use.
