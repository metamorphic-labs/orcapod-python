### Breaking changes and migration

- `DBConnectorProtocol` has three more required methods: `table_name(path)`, `has_table(table_name)` and `table_paths(prefix, pending)`. `ConnectorArrowDatabase` now asks the connector to name its tables, test their existence and list their paths. SQLite, PostgreSQL and SpiralDB keep the names and listings they had. A connector of your own implements the three methods; `orcapod.databases.table_names` has the rule the built-in connectors use.
- `DBConnectorProtocol.validate_records` takes a required keyword argument, `table_name` (the name `table_name(path)` gave, or `None` for rows checked for no table in particular). `ConnectorArrowDatabase` passes it, so a connector that records something per table checks the rows against that table before they are buffered: a SpiralDB store refuses rows of another data context than the table records. SQLite and PostgreSQL ignore it. A connector of your own adds the argument.
