### Added

- `job.register(*streams, **named_streams)` (also on `Pipeline`) brings streams built while
  exploring into a job: each comes in with its pod and every upstream step down to its
  sources, so the job has the same nodes, hashes, stored tables and results as the same
  pipeline written in a `with` block. A keyword names that stream's node. A node of another
  job is taken in with its steps; a step of a loaded job whose function is not attached is
  refused, naming the step, with a pointer to `as_source()` for its stored results.

### Changed

- A stream built before a job and used by a step inside a `with` block comes in with its
  pod and every upstream step, as `register` does. It used to become a source slot named
  `<label>:<hash>` that stored none of its steps (or was refused when it lay over a join). A
  stream becomes a source only when you make it one: a source object, or a node's
  `as_source()`.
- `SourceNode.from_stream` and `SourceJobNode.from_stream` need an explicit `name` for a
  stream made by a pod.
- A `with` block whose body or compilation raises leaves the job as it was when the block
  was entered (its recorded steps, its nodes and its bindings); a refused step no longer
  stays recorded and fails the next block.
