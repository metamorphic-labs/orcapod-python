### Fixed

- A chain of operators built before a job is opened, with a step over it added inside the
  `with` block, gives the same nodes, system-tag column names, pipeline hashes and stored
  tables as the same chain built inside the block (ITL-1470). The stream used to become a
  source slot, which named its system-tag columns by its own schema and had its own
  pipeline hash, while its content hash was that of the chain. The change that fixes it is
  ITL-1387 (a stream given to a job brings its upstream steps); this entry adds the test
  that compares the two forms, on the sync and the async path.
