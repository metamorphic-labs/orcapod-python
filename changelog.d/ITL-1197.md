### Added

- **The pipeline graph in a notebook, without Graphviz (in development).** A `Pipeline` or `PipelineJob` shown at the end of a notebook cell now draws its DAG: sources, operators, function pods and side-effect pods as nodes, streams as edges, laid out left to right. Where the notebook runs scripts, the diagram can be dragged and zoomed, and selecting a node shows its tag and data columns and, for a job, its status (`not run`, `current`, `held`, `stale`, `failed`, `partial`, `loaded only`) and the row counts of the last run. `pipeline.diagram()` and `job.diagram()` make it with options: `direction="TB"`, `collapse=True` (fold joins and column selections, drops and maps into the edges), `max_nodes`. `print(job.diagram())` lists the graph as text, and `job.diagram().save(path)` writes a standalone page. Drawing computes nothing: it runs no function, reads no source and no database. It needs no Graphviz, no new dependency and nothing from the network; the picture stays in a saved notebook and an HTML export, and shows (without pan, zoom or selection) where scripts do not run. `Pipeline.show_graph()` is unchanged. The diagram is in development: its interface, its look and the notebook display may change in any release without a deprecation period; the view carries a small "preview" tag. (ITL-1197)

### Fixed

- **A `tags_as_data` source no longer reads its origin to give its columns.** `output_schema()` and `keys()` of a `DerivedSource` built with `tags_as_data` (such as `node.as_source(tags_as_data=...)`) derive the columns from the origin's zero-row table when the source holds no rows, instead of reading the origin's stored rows (and reading them again on every call while the origin had stored nothing). Showing a job built on such a source, or predicting a downstream node's schema, now reads no database. (ITL-1197)

### Known limitations

- **The notebook diagram is in development.** `Pipeline.diagram()`, `PipelineJob.diagram()`, `PipelineDiagram` with its methods and arguments, the model `to_dict()` returns, the look of the picture and the notebook display of `Pipeline` and `PipelineJob` may change in any release without a deprecation period. (ITL-1197)
