### Added

- **The pipeline graph in a notebook, without Graphviz.** A `Pipeline` or `PipelineJob` shown at the end of a notebook cell now draws its DAG: sources, operators, function pods and side-effect pods as nodes, streams as edges, laid out left to right. Where the notebook runs scripts, the diagram can be dragged and zoomed, and selecting a node shows its tag and data columns and, for a job, its status (`not run`, `current`, `held`, `stale`, `failed`, `loaded only`) and the row counts of the last run. `pipeline.diagram()` and `job.diagram()` make it with options: `direction="TB"`, `collapse=True` (fold joins and column selections, drops and maps into the edges), `max_nodes`. `print(job.diagram())` lists the graph as text, and `job.diagram().save(path)` writes a standalone page. Drawing computes nothing: it runs no function, reads no source and no database. It needs no Graphviz, no new dependency and nothing from the network; the picture stays in a saved notebook and an HTML export, and shows (without pan, zoom or selection) where scripts do not run. `Pipeline.show_graph()` is unchanged. (ITL-1197)
