### Added

- `SpiralDBTableSource.where(...)` and `where=` take ranges of integer, timestamp and date
  columns, written with `col` as in `filter`: `events.where(col("timestamp") >= start,
  col("timestamp") < stop)`, and `col(c).between(a, b)` (both ends included). SpiralDB
  applies them in the scan, with any equalities; all must hold. A range is a restriction:
  no hash, source id, schema or record id changes. Bounds are checked like equality values
  and never converted, rounded or clamped. A range of a float, string, binary or boolean
  column is refused and points to `filter` (SpiralDB orders floats by IEEE 754 total order,
  not as Orcapod does; ITL-1477).
- `SpiralDBTableSource.where` takes several conditions as positional arguments, as `filter`
  and `Condition.of` do: mappings and conditions, plus keywords.

### Changed

- A condition made only of equalities (a source's `where`, a `Filter`) is saved with its
  columns in canonical order, as a condition with other terms already was: `where(b=1,
  a=2)` and `where(a=2, b=1)` save the same bytes. Saved jobs and configs written before
  load to the same condition; their bytes change only when two or more columns had been
  given out of that order. No hash, stored path or identity reads a saved condition.
- `SpiralDBConnector.scan_batches(where=...)` takes a `Condition` (equalities and ranges)
  instead of a mapping: wrap a mapping in `Condition.of(...)`.
- A condition that requires two different values of one column in `where` now raises
  `ValueError` ("already restricted"), as across chained calls, rather than `TypeError`.
