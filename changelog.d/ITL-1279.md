### Changed

- **Nine more operators are provisional:** `GroupBy`, `TagBy`, `AddTags`, `MergeJoin`,
  `MapTags`, `MapColumns`, `Pick`, `Index` and `Batch`, beside `PolarsFilter`
  (`orcapod.operators.PROVISIONAL_OPERATORS`). Their identities are not frozen with data
  context v1: a later release may change one, and then the steps built on it compute their
  results again instead of reusing them (nothing stored is lost or wrong). Building one,
  directly, through a stream method (`stream.group_by`, `stream.add_tags`, ...) or by
  loading a saved job, issues `ProvisionalHashWarning` once per operator per process; to
  silence it, `warnings.filterwarnings("ignore", category=orcapod.errors.ProvisionalHashWarning)`.
  Every other operator is frozen: `Join`, `SemiJoin`, `MapData`, the column selections and
  `DropNulls`. No operator's identity or output changes. (ITL-1279)
- **The golden values of the provisional library value types are no longer frozen with v1:**
  NumPy arrays, torch tensors and pandas and Polars frames and series, and a user class with a
  field of one of them, whose identity embeds that type's identifier (ITL-1324). No value
  changes.

### Known limitations

- **`DerivedSource` is provisional, in both forms:** `node.as_source()` (and
  `DerivedSource(origin)`) and `DerivedSource(origin, tags_as_data=...)` are in
  `orcapod.sources.PROVISIONAL_SOURCES` beside `PollingSource`. Their source ids and hashes
  are not frozen with data context v1, so a later release may change them, and every step
  built on one then computes its results again instead of reusing them (nothing stored is
  lost or wrong). Building one, also through `node.as_source()`, from a saved config or by
  loading a saved job, issues `ProvisionalHashWarning` once per process. No id, hash, config
  or stored path changes, and their golden values moved, unchanged, to the provisional
  golden files. (ITL-1331)
