### Fixed

- `GroupBy("subject_id")` and `stream.group_by("subject_id")` group on the column
  `subject_id`; they used to group on the columns `'s'`, `'u'`, `'b'`, ... (a one-character
  name worked by accident). A bare string is one column name for every operator that takes
  column names, through one rule (`schema_utils.column_names`); `GroupBy("x")` has the
  identity of `GroupBy(["x"])`, and the identity of a list or tuple argument is unchanged.
  `bytes` and non-string names are refused with `TypeError`.
