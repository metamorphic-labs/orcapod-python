### Changed

- **A job shows a source by its own label.** Where the source bound to a slot has an assigned label, the job's diagram, the node's preview and `RestrictedAggregationWarning` name it by that label instead of its slot's name; the diagram's details and the warning give the full source id. Sources sharing a label, or a label that is another node's name, are shown as `label (part)`, with the shortest part that tells them apart (a SpiralDB table's dataset, then its project, then the source's name). Display only: slot names, node labels, the keys of `job.nodes`, saved files, hashes, ids and stored paths are unchanged, and so is the text of a source without a label and of a loaded job. (ITL-1454)

### Fixed

- **`UnrecordedTableSchemaWarning` holds no lock while it warns.** A warning hook may wait for another thread that reads an unrecorded SpiralDB table, or read the table itself. The warning is usually shown once per table and process: threads reading a table at the same time may each show it, and once shown it is not shown again. Turned into an error, it is raised by every reader. (ITL-1454)
