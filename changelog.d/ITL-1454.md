### Changed

- **A job shows a source by its own label.** Where the source bound to a slot has an assigned label, the job's diagram, the node's preview, and the text of `explain()`, `trace()` and `RestrictedAggregationWarning` name it by that label instead of its slot's name or source id; the diagram's details and the texts give the full source id once. Sources sharing a label, or a label that is another node's name, are shown as `label (part)`, with the shortest part that tells them apart (a SpiralDB table's dataset, then its project, then the source's name). Display only: slot names, node labels, the keys of `job.nodes`, saved files, hashes, ids and stored paths are unchanged, and so is the text of a source without a label and of a loaded job.
