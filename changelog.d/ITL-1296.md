### Changed

- **`PolarsFilter` warns every time one is built; `Filter` is the filter to use**
  (ITL-1296). `PolarsFilter` passes every column of its input through Polars, also the
  columns its predicates do not read, and Polars does not hold every Arrow type exactly: a
  `date64` value that is not at midnight comes back at midnight, with no error. That fault
  is not fixed in this release. Building a `PolarsFilter` (the class, or
  `stream.polars_filter(...)`) now issues the new `PolarsFilterWarning` (from
  `orcapod.errors`) at every construction, in addition to the once-per-process
  `ProvisionalHashWarning`; loading a saved job issues it once for each `PolarsFilter`
  the job holds, at every load. Use `Filter` (`stream.filter(...)`), which passes every value
  through as it is; the documentation now presents it as the filter. To silence the
  warning: `warnings.filterwarnings("ignore", category=PolarsFilterWarning)`. No hash,
  identity or stored path changes.

### Known limitations

- `PolarsFilter` can change the values of the rows it keeps (a `date64` off midnight is
  moved to midnight), and a `decimal256` column can make Polars abort the process
  (ITL-1296). Use `Filter`.
