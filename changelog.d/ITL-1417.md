### Fixed

- A saved and loaded job could run `MergeJoin` or `SemiJoin` with its two inputs swapped
  when another step over its second input had been recorded first: the loader took an
  operator's inputs in the order the steps were recorded. A saved job now lists each
  operator's inputs in argument order (`inputs` in the node's entry), and loading uses that
  list. A job file saved before this fix that holds `MergeJoin` or `SemiJoin` over two
  inputs is refused at load, with a message to save it again from its code; a file with only
  commutative operators (`Join`, `Rejoin`) loads as before.
