### Fixed

- A saved and loaded job could run `MergeJoin` or `SemiJoin` with its two inputs swapped
  when another step over its second input had been recorded first: the loader took an
  operator's inputs in the order the steps were recorded. A saved job now lists each
  operator's inputs in argument order (`inputs` in the node's entry), and loading uses that
  list. A job file saved before this fix loads an operator over two or more inputs only when
  the operator is known to be commutative (`Join`, `Rejoin`, or a custom operator whose
  `argument_symmetry` on its inputs is a flat `frozenset` of them); any other (`MergeJoin`,
  `SemiJoin`, an operator kept read-only on load) is refused with a message to save the job
  again from its code. Loaded read-only nodes keep their inputs.
