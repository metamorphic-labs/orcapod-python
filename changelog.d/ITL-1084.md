### Added

- `stream.drop_columns(...)` and `stream.select_columns(...)`, with the operators `DropColumns` and `SelectColumns`, act on named columns whether each is a tag or a data column (ITL-1084). `drop_columns` removes the named columns; `select_columns` keeps exactly the named columns and removes every other tag and data column. The names are given as one or more strings or as one collection of strings: `stream.drop_columns("a", "b")` is `stream.drop_columns(["a", "b"])`; `strict` and `label` are keyword-only. For names of one group they give the rows of `DropTagColumns`, `DropDataColumns`, `SelectTagColumns` and `SelectDataColumns`, which are unchanged, as are their hashes. Unlike those four, a call whose result would have no data column raises `InputValidationError` at once, on the sync and async paths, and no meta (`__`) column of the input is carried, as with `Join`.
