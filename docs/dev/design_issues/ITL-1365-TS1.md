## Cross-cutting

### TS1 — A stream's tag schema followed its table layout, not its tag order
**Status:** resolved
**Severity:** medium
**Issue:** ITL-1365

`ArrowTableStream` built its tag schema (and its system-tag schema) from its table's
columns in table order, while its `keys()` list the tags in the declared tag order and its
data schema follows `keys()` too. For a table not laid out in tag order (a source over
columns `a, b, c, x` with `tag_columns=["c", "b", "a"]`), `output_schema()[0]` gave
`a, b, c` and `keys()[0]` gave `c, b, a`. Every operator predicts its schema from its
input's `output_schema()`, so its predicted `keys()` and `output_schema()` listed the
tags in another order than its computed result, its rows (sync and async) and its own
input: `DropTagColumns(["a"])` predicted `("b", "c")` and computed `("c", "b")` (192 of
1,536 cases of the reporter's sweep). It was not only `DropTagColumns`: on main almost
every unary operator's predicted keys disagreed with its rows in such a layout
(ITL-1249, symptom A). No value, row, stored table or hash differed: only the order.

**Fix:** `ArrowTableStream.__init__` builds the tag and system-tag schemas from the tag
columns in tag order, as the data schema already followed the data columns. What
changes for a user: `output_schema()` lists the tags of such a stream (every source kind
over such a table) in tag order, and so does every prediction downstream; a saved job
writes those tag schemas in that order. What does not change, measured by
`docs/dev/scripts/itl1365_tag_schema_order.py` over every source kind and a job before
and after: rows, every table's column order, content and pipeline hashes, source ids,
stored paths and stored column order, and the golden values; a job saved before loads
with the same hashes and stored rows. Left open (ITL-1249): a data-tag that loses its
tag role (`DropTagColumns`, `SelectTagColumns`, a `GroupBy` non-key) is predicted
right after the data-tags but sits at its table position in the result.
