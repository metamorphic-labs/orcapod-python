## `src/orcapod/pipeline/base.py` — node names of a pipeline or job

### NO1 — A job's node names follow recording order
**Status:** open
**Severity:** medium
**Issue:** ITL-1389

A node's name is its key in `job.nodes`, its attribute name and its key in a saved job. Two
ways make it follow the order in which steps were recorded, against the rule that order never
counts unless it is given (ITL-1271):

- **Suffixes are given at every compile.** A label held by several nodes becomes
  `<label>_1`, `<label>_2`, ... in content-hash order, recomputed by every `compile()`. A job
  with one unlabelled `MapData` that gains a second one (by reopening `with job:` or by
  `register`) has `MapData_1` and `MapData_2`: the first node is renamed. The names never
  depend on the order within one compile, but they are not stable as the job grows.
- **One step under two labels keeps the last recorded.** The same step (one content hash)
  recorded in one `with` block as `label="x"` and then `label="y"` gives one node named `y`;
  in the other order, `x` (the later invocation overwrites the earlier). `register` and the
  walk of ITL-1387 refuse a step reached under two different assigned labels; the `with`
  block's own direct recording still keeps the last.

Labels enter no hash and no stored path. Found while designing ITL-1387
(`docs/dev/specs/itl-1387-job-from-streams.md`, sections 1.2 and 1.4).
