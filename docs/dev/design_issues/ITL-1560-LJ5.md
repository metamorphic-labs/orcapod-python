## `src/orcapod/pipeline/job.py`

### LJ5 — Capturing steps or recompiling erased the graph of a loaded job
**Status:** resolved
**Severity:** high
**Issue:** ITL-1560

`PipelineJob.load` restores the compiled graph without the invocations that built it.
Capturing a step in the loaded job's context replaced the restored graph with the new
branch. Calling `compile()` directly rebuilt from the empty invocation record and discarded
the restored nodes.

**Fix:** As a stopgap, a loaded job refuses capture and recompilation before altering
its graph or bindings. It still supports binding and load-only runs. Support for editing
the graph of a loaded job is a separate design question (ITL-1559).
